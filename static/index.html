Support Guides
Do I need to install anything?

No, everything runs in the browser. The SteamShowcase Helper extension only speeds up uploading to Steam.

Why does Steam reject my GIF?

Steam accepts files up to 5 MB and needs the HEX 21 byte. Showcase Maker does both; for files made elsewhere use the HEX tab.

Do you need my Steam password?

No. The site never asks for it. The extension works inside your own Steam session in the browser, and nothing changes on your profile until you press Save on Steam.

Are my files public?

No. Sources and ZIPs are deleted automatically after their storage time; a preview becomes public only if you share its link yourself.

How does paying for Pro work?

You pay once for a term, with no auto-renewal. Days add up with the Pro you already have. Pro is bound to your account and turns on by itself after payment.

Is this made by Valve?

No. It is an independent project; Steam and Valve are trademarks of Valve Corporation.
